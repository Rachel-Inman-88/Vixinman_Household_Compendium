## How the NM AHJ system works (unchanged, verified)

New Mexico uses a two-tier system. The **Construction Industries Division (CID)** of the Regulation & Licensing Department is the default Authority Having Jurisdiction for all unincorporated areas and for municipalities without certified building departments. **Local Enforcement Agencies (LEAs)** — chiefly Albuquerque, Santa Fe, Las Cruces, Rio Rancho, and Farmington — run their own building departments for work inside their limits. CID retains authority over **trade licensing** statewide regardless of who issues the permit. Some counties (Santa Fe, Taos, Rio Arriba, Bernalillo, San Juan, McKinley, Grant, Lincoln) additionally run county development/building-permit functions for unincorporated areas.

**Tribal and pueblo lands:** CID and LEA codes do NOT apply. Contact tribal building officials directly. Navajo Nation Tribal Building Codes: ⚠ VERIFY 928-871-6376. NTUA (electric utility on Navajo Nation): ✅ 800-528-5011.

**Federal land and installations** (national forests, Holloman AFB, etc.): CID jurisdiction does not apply — contact the federal land manager or base civil engineering.
