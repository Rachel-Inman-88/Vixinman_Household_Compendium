⏱ Overtime rule

Once someone passes this many hours of overtime-eligible time in a week, the extra hours automatically earn the multiplier — nobody logs overtime as its own type.

Pay types {{ ns.active }}

A multiplier type pays the employee's base wage × the multiplier. A flat type pays a fixed $/hr regardless of base wage. "Counts toward OT" = these hours add up toward the weekly overtime threshold (turn it off for PTO/Holiday). "Leave" = vacation/PTO/sick — these hours can't take a week past the overtime threshold (they can't earn OT); only the GM can override that on the approval form. Values are the defaults; override them per person below.
