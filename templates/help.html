1. Signing in & your account

↑ contents

Tutorial — set up self-service password reset

  1. Click your name (top right) → My account.
  2. Under Security questions, pick 3 questions and type answers only you know.
  3. Confirm with your current password and Save. Answers are stored scrambled and are case-sensitive.
I forgot my password — how do I get back in?

On the sign-in page click Forgot your password?, enter your username, and answer the 2 (randomly chosen) security questions you enrolled. You then set a new password yourself — no admin needed. If you never set up questions, ask a manager to reset it from your employee profile.

Why did my account get locked after a reset attempt?

Too many wrong security-question answers auto-locks the account for safety and notifies a Supervisor (or the GM). A manager reinstates it from your employee profile.

I got signed out on my own — why?

Sessions end after 12 hours of inactivity (the clock resets on each action). Just sign in again.

2. The idea backlog

↑ contents

Tutorial — add an idea and start it as a project

  1. From 🗄 Databases → Backlog, add an idea — a repair, build, or certification not started yet.
  2. Optionally set who proposed it, a target/someday date, a rough budget estimate, and a custom reminder date.
  3. When you're ready, open the idea and click 🚀 Start as a project — it becomes a real project you fill in from there.
What happens to an idea I'm not doing anymore?

Mark it Abandoned from the idea's page — it stays on record (not deleted) and can be moved back to the backlog later if you change your mind.

How do backlog reminders work?

Once a month, everyone with a login gets a single notification if anything is still sitting in the backlog. You can also set a custom reminder date on a specific idea for a one-time nudge on top of that.

3. Projects & the pipeline

↑ contents

Stages: Planning → Prep → In Progress → Wrap-up → Done (plus Abandoned). Each stage is owned by a department with exit criteria.

Tutorial — advance a project

  1. Open the project. The Pipeline stage panel shows who owns it and what's needed to advance.
  2. Use Advance to … when ready. Prep auto-advances to In Progress once permits are filed and you set an install date.
  3. To change the stage directly, click the ✎ next to the stage name.

Tutorial — cancel (and reopen) a project

  1. On the project, open 🚫 Cancel this project, type a reason (required), and confirm. The project is marked Abandoned.
  2. Its open tasks stop showing in My Tasks / the board / the Work Bag, and everyone who was involved is notified.
  3. To bring it back, use ↩ Reopen project — it returns to the exact stage it was at.
Where do cancelled projects go?

Nowhere is deleted. A GM/Admin can review them under 🗄 Databases → Closed projects (reason, who/when, contract) with one-click Reopen, alongside completed projects.

Who gets told when a project moves to a new stage?

The department(s) that own the new stage get an inbox notification. Your copy clears the first time you open it (or open the project).

4. Loads, sizing & the bill of materials (BOM)

↑ contents

Tutorial — record a load survey & size the system

  1. On the project, open ⚡ Loads & Sizing. Loads are recorded here during the walkthrough — not at project creation.
  2. Add rooms/appliances (each room's picker defaults to its type; use Custom for off-catalog items). 🟢 Modern / 🟠 Vintage tags help.
  3. In Designer mode, use component auto-suggest to add PV modules, batteries, and the inverter to the BOM at the right quantity.
Why can't I edit the loads anymore?

Loads & Sizing is a Planning-phase tool. Once the contract is signed (the project moves past Planning) the figures lock and become view-only.

What's the difference between the BOM and the Packing list?

The BOM is the priced bill of materials (equipment) built in Loads & Sizing. The 📦 Packing list in the Work Bag is the field checklist of materials to load on the truck.

5. Rules, licenses, permits & compliance (L/P/C)

↑ contents

Tutorial — add or edit a requirement rule

  1. Go to 🗄 Databases → Rules Editor.
  2. Pick the project field + value that triggers it, a category, and a label; add links, phone, notes, and the verbatim source text if it's a compliance rule.
  3. Set the Verification callout dropdown (none / ⚠ Verify / ⚠ Unverified) to flag anything staff should confirm — you can add or remove it any time.
Where do the requirements show up on a project?

On the project's L/P/C tab (Permits lead, with an inline upload slot each), and browsable in the read-only L/P/C Directory filtered by project type.

What does the ⚠ Verify chip mean?

It's a reminder to confirm that item before relying on it (Unverified = couldn't confirm online). It's an editable field per rule now, not automatic.

6. Tasks & the field Work Bag

↑ contents

Tutorial — do field work from the Work Bag

  1. Open 🎒 Work Bag (works offline). Tap a project to see its field tasks, hours, receipts, and notes.
  2. Finish a task with ✓ Submit as done and enter the time it took, split by pay type (e.g. 8h regular + 1h travel + 2h roof).
  3. Photo steps open a take / review / submit screen — at least one photo is required.
  4. Your supervisor approves the task, then Finance approves the hours for payroll (two sign-offs).
Can I log a receipt or a note from the field?

Yes — snap a receipt (date, total, vendor, category) and it's filed as a paid expense; the 📝 Project notes box saves timestamped notes for the office.

My tasks list looks empty for a project I know exists.

Cancelled (Abandoned) projects are hidden from tasks. Also, the field views show only on-site work; office/scheduling steps live on your dashboard.

6b. Boards — stand-alone to-dos

↑ contents

Boards (📋 in the top bar) are to-dos not tied to a project — clean the bathroom, call a vendor, etc.

Tutorial — create a to-do and send it to a teammate

  1. Open 📋 Boards and use ＋ New board — give it a title, optionally a person to send it to, a priority, and a due date.
  2. Open the board to log time (hours + date + note, with a running total), add notes as it progresses, and change its status.
  3. To hand it off, use Send this to-do to and pick a teammate — they get a notification.
Where do I see to-dos sent to me?

On 📋 Boards, the Mine filter shows to-dos assigned to you; you also get a 🔔 notification when one is sent to you.

Can I update an older board's notes later?

Yes — open the board any time and add another note; the log keeps every entry with author and timestamp.

7. Finance, billing & pricing

↑ contents

Tutorial — price a project with the estimate

  1. On the project, open the 📐 Estimate tab (Finance/Sales/Design) and click Prefill from cost model.
  2. Set quantities for Labor / Travel / Adders / mounts; equipment pulls from the BOM. Overhead (G&A) applies automatically.
  3. Use Use suggested as contract total to set the contract.

Tutorial — track a project's budget

  1. On the 💵 Billing tab, set the contract total (and GRT if applicable).
  2. Record deposits, receipts, and expenses as income/expense ledger entries as the project goes.
  3. The Billing tab totals up contract, collected, outstanding, and expenses so you can see where the budget stands.
Where do markups, county tax, and travel rates live?

🗄 Databases → Cost model & finance (Finance/Admin/GM): six cost-model sections (equipment markup, labor, travel, adders, overhead) plus the NM county GRT table.

Who can see the cost/margin breakdown?

The internal Pricing breakdown on the Billing tab is visible to Finance, Sales & Design only.

How does payroll work?

Crews log hours by pay type in the Work Bag; supervisors approve them; Finance runs payroll (Sun→Sat periods, automatic overtime, QuickBooks CSV export). Only the GM and Payroll Manager change pay rates.

8. Inventory, barcodes & stock audit

↑ contents

Tutorial — mark an item stale

  1. Open 🗄 Databases → Inventory and find the item.
  2. Click the 🕰 toggle in its row to flag it stale (click again to clear). It shows a Stale badge and joins the review queue.

Tutorial — run a stock audit

  1. On Inventory, choose 🧮 Audit stock and start a session (all stock or one category).
  2. Scan every tag (phone camera or scanner). Compendium reconciles against what it expects to be in stock.
  3. Finish to get a report of unaccounted / unexpected / unknown / duplicate items — exportable as CSV.
How do barcodes / asset tags work?

The Warehouse Manager registers serials and prints Code-128 labels; anyone can scan items in/out to load a truck (including from a phone). Two installers can load the same project at once.

9. People, roles & permissions

↑ contents

Tutorial — onboard a new employee

  1. Add them under 👥 Team → Employees (roles, and a login if they need one).
  2. Open their profile → Onboarding tab and check off steps as they're completed. Edit the checklist template from the Employees page.
How do the dashboard "modes" work?

Your dashboard shows a section per department you belong to; the View: buttons focus on one. Your default is remembered. (Sales-role users see a 🏁 Wrap-up mode where installers see Installation.)

What is "emergency revoke access"?

A GM or a designated Supervisor can instantly suspend all of an employee's access from their profile — they're signed out and blocked until reinstated. Nothing is deleted.

How are permissions granted?

The GM has full access and can grant individuals specific tools (optionally with an expiry). Admin sits below GM; most people get exactly what their role needs.

10. Notifications

↑ contents

The 🔔 in the top bar is your inbox (the badge is your unread count). You'll get notifications for pipeline turnovers to your department, project cancellations you were involved in, and security alerts. Opening a notification clears it for you.

A notification disappeared — where did it go?

They clear once accessed (by opening the notification or the project it points to). Use Clear all on the inbox to empty it.

11. For managers & admins

↑ contents
  • 🔧 Admin → Log: the audit log of every change (who/what/when), with passwords redacted.
  • 🔧 Admin → Trash: deleted items (GM restores or purges). Deletes are blocked if data is in use.
  • 🔧 Admin → Access: grant per-person tool access, optionally with an expiry (GM only).
  • 🗄 Databases → Closed projects: review cancelled + completed projects; reopen a cancelled project.
  • 🗄 Databases → Cost model & finance: markups, county GRT, travel, overhead.
  • Executive dashboard: company overview — pipeline counts, money in flight, attention row, ready-for-design, this week's installs, and the Wrap-up worklist.
How do we back up the data?

Everything lives in job_creator.db with uploaded files in the uploads/ folder — back up both together. In the desktop app that's your personal Compendium folder.
